# Wednesday 18 November 2026 — in one image

A front-page infographic summing up the world's news of Wednesday 18 November 2026:

- **Iran's drones target US radar in Bahrain** — IRGC drones at Isa Air Base intercepted; US jets hit back on Qeshm Island
- **Quad meets in Manila** — first leaders' summit in two years; joint patrol-aircraft drills agreed
- **Dow plunges 1,000 points** — Dow −849 to 50,759; S&P 500 lowest close since late July
- **Nvidia's record $115.8bn, no Huang return** — revenue +103%; board rules out reinstating Jensen Huang
- **UK sanctions eight settlement firms** — asset freezes on West Bank builders and Gaza aid-blocking group Tzav 9
- **Canada and China cut tariffs in Shenzhen** — relief on ~C$400m of Canadian pork and seafood

The image was drawn from scratch in this session with `draw_entry.py`
(Python + PIL, Liberation fonts, no external images) and visually checked
for legibility and overflow. `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only)
copies `entry.png` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` (or `/output/entry.png`).
The image is a 1024×1024 PNG, 146,456 bytes — inside the 1 MiB / 1024×1024 limits.
